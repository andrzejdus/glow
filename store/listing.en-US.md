## Full description

```text
Glow is an unofficial Bluetooth remote for compatible portable and desktop devices. It shows the temperature, switches the heater on and off, and puts every device setting on your phone, with no account and no internet connection.

FEATURES

• Reconnects on its own. Glow remembers your device and connects as soon as you open the app: no device picker and no Connect button after the first time.
• Dashboard. A temperature dial that glows while the device heats and turns green when the target is reached, a status line that mirrors the device's LED, time to ready, and the Booster and Superbooster levels.
• Hours of operation. The device's lifetime counter, counting up live, with the hours per month.
• Battery. Level, charging and charged.
• Workflows on the VOLCANO HYBRID. Run its built-in step programs from the phone, step by step, with the time running and the time left.
• Settings. The device settings the maker's own web app offers, such as LED brightness, vibration, automatic shutoff, charging options and Find My, plus the Booster and Superbooster offsets. Firmware update and factory reset are left out on purpose.
• Device diagnostics. Live raw registers with changed bits highlighted and a change log you can copy.
• Demo mode. "Try without a device" on the Connect screen simulates a device, so you can look around first.
• Light and dark theme, following your phone.

PRIVACY

Glow runs entirely on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no server. It talks to your device over Bluetooth and makes no network requests. The only things it keeps, on the phone, are the device you last used and its hours-of-operation history.
Privacy policy: https://github.com/andrzejdus/glow/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

COMPATIBILITY

Works with these STORZ & BICKEL Bluetooth vaporizers: VENTY, VEAZY, CRAFTY+, CRAFTY and VOLCANO HYBRID. So far only the CRAFTY+ has been tried on a real device; support for the others is built from the protocol the maker's web app uses and covered by automated tests. MIGHTY and MIGHTY+ are not supported. Needs Android 7.0 or newer with Bluetooth Low Energy.

Glow is intended for adults.

OPEN SOURCE

Glow is free and open source. The code, release notes and issue tracker are at https://github.com/andrzejdus/glow

Glow is not made, endorsed or supported by STORZ & BICKEL. STORZ & BICKEL, VENTY, VEAZY, CRAFTY, VOLCANO and MIGHTY are trademarks of their owner and are used here only to describe compatibility.
```
